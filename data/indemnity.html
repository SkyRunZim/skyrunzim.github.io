IMPORTANT NOTICE: This is a legally binding agreement containing an acknowledgment of serious risks, an assumption of risk, a release and an indemnity in favour of Wild Goat Co (Pvt) Ltd as the sole event organiser. Participation may result in serious injury, permanent disability or death. Read the whole document before signing. Nothing in this agreement excludes any right or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded under Zimbabwean law.

Acknowledgment of Risk and Release of Liability

1. Understanding and acknowledgment of risk

I understand that SkyRun Zimbabwe is a remote, strenuous mountain trail event. The risks include, but are not limited to:

  • prolonged physical exertion, exhaustion, dehydration, heat illness, hypothermia, altitude effects and other medical emergencies;
  • steep, uneven, slippery or unstable terrain, cliffs, falls, collisions, river or water crossings and natural obstacles;
  • rapidly changing or extreme weather, darkness, poor visibility, lightning, fire and environmental hazards;
  • navigation errors, becoming lost or separated, equipment failure, vehicle or transport incidents and wildlife encounters; and
  • limited communications, remote access and delayed rescue, evacuation or medical treatment.

I understand that this list is not exhaustive and that unexpected risks may arise.

2. Participant responsibilities

I will provide accurate information, prepare appropriately, carry the mandatory equipment, follow course GPX track and all reasonable instructions from organisers, marshals and medical personnel, assist where reasonably able, and report any withdrawal. I will not participate while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs or medication that makes participation unsafe. I accept that race officials may delay, reroute, stop or remove me from the event when reasonably necessary for safety.

3. Medical fitness and disclosure

I confirm that I am sufficiently fit and prepared to participate and that I have disclosed any relevant medical condition, allergy, medication or disability that may affect my safety or emergency treatment. I understand that the organisers do not provide a medical assessment and that I should obtain independent medical advice if I am uncertain about my fitness.

4. Assumption of risk release and indemnity

I voluntarily accept the inherent and other risks described in this agreement. To the fullest extent permitted by Zimbabwean law, I release and hold harmless Wild Goat Co (Pvt) Ltd as organiser, its event partners Far and Wide Zimbabwe (Pvt) Ltd and Mutarazi SkyWalk (Pvt) Ltd, and their respective directors, employees, volunteers, contractors, agents and sponsors (the Protected Parties) from claims for personal injury, illness, death, property loss or damage arising from those accepted risks or from my own acts, omissions or breach of this agreement. I indemnify the Protected Parties against third-party claims caused by my negligent or wrongful conduct or breach. This clause does not exclude liability or statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

5. Rules and event changes

I will comply with published event rules and reasonable safety instructions. I understand that the route, start time or event may be altered, postponed, shortened or cancelled when reasonably necessary because of weather, safety, emergencies or circumstances outside the organiser's reasonable control. Any refund or transfer will be governed by the separately published entry terms and applicable law.

6. Emergency treatment and evacuation

If I am injured, ill, unconscious or otherwise unable to decide, I authorise the organiser, marshals, medical personnel and emergency services to provide reasonable first aid and arrange rescue, evacuation, transport and medical treatment considered reasonably necessary. I accept responsibility, to the fullest extent permitted by law, for reasonable evacuation, transport and treatment costs not paid by the organiser or an insurer. I understand that emergency response may be delayed by terrain, weather, distance or communications limitations.

7. Personal information and medical data consent

I consent in writing to Wild Goat Co (Pvt) Ltd collecting and using the personal, health and emergency-contact information I provide for entry administration, participant identification, event safety, emergency response, legal compliance and the establishment or defence of legal claims. Relevant information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with event partners, timing and registration providers, medical personnel, emergency services and authorities. Information must be kept secure and retained no longer than reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law. I may request access or correction and may withdraw consent where the law permits, although withdrawal may prevent participation where the information is necessary for safety.

8. Photography and media

I consent to identifiable photographs and video of me being used by Wild Goat Co (Pvt) Ltd and the named event partners for event reporting and promotional purposes, without payment.

9. Participants under 18

A participant under 18 must have this agreement completed and signed by a parent or legal guardian. The parent or guardian confirms authority to consent, agrees to the provisions on the minor's behalf to the extent permitted by law, authorises emergency treatment, and accepts responsibility for ensuring that the minor understands and follows event rules.

10. Legal terms

This agreement is governed by the laws of Zimbabwe. If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, it will be limited or severed only to the extent necessary and the remaining provisions will continue to apply. This indemnity concerns participation risk and does not replace the event's separate entry, cancellation or refund terms. No wording in this document limits any non-waivable consumer or other statutory right.

Declaration

By signing up for SkyRun Zimbabwe, and signing in to the SkyRun app I confirm that I received this agreement before participating, had enough time to read it, could ask questions, understand its nature and effect, and enter into this event voluntarily.
